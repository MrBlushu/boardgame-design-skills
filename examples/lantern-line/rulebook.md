# Lantern Line

`Lantern Line` is an original cooperative microgame created to demonstrate the audit workflow. This draft intentionally contains rules defects.

## Goal

Complete the red, blue, and gold lantern paths before the storm marker reaches space 6.

## Components

- 18 Lantern cards: values 1–6 in red, blue, and gold;
- 6 Storm cards;
- 1 Storm track;
- 1 storm marker;
- 3 Path cards.

## Setup

Place the three Path cards in the center of the table. Put the storm marker on space 0. Shuffle all Lantern cards and deal three cards to each player. A player can never hold more than three cards. The youngest player goes first.

Lantern Line supports two to four players.

## Turn

Play proceeds clockwise. On your turn:

1. Draw until you have four cards.
2. Play one Lantern card on the matching path. Its value must be higher than the previous card. A value 1 card can start any empty path.
3. If you cannot play a card, discard one card and advance the storm marker one space.
4. Refill your hand to three cards.

Players may discuss their cards, but hands must remain secret.

## Completed paths

A path is complete when its value 6 card is played. Completed paths remain in play.

## End of the game

The players win immediately when all three paths are complete. The players lose immediately when the storm marker reaches space 6.

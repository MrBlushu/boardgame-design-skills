# Lantern Line — revised rules

This revision resolves the defects in the deliberately flawed demonstration draft. It is an original cooperative microgame used to demonstrate playtest planning.

## Goal

Complete the red, blue, and gold lantern paths before the storm marker reaches space 6.

## Components

- 18 Lantern cards: one card of each value from 1 to 6 in red, blue, and gold;
- 1 Storm track with spaces 0–6;
- 1 storm marker;
- 3 Path cards: red, blue, and gold.

## Setup

Place the three Path cards in the center of the table. Put the storm marker on space 0. Shuffle the Lantern cards and deal three cards to each player. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw deck. The youngest player goes first.

Lantern Line supports two to four players. A hand may contain at most four cards.

## Communication

Players may discuss strategy, paths, and risk. They may not show cards or communicate information about the identity of a card in any hand, including its color or value. Cards on paths and in the discard pile are face up and public, and players may discuss them.

## Turn

Play proceeds clockwise.

1. If the draw deck is not empty, draw one card. Otherwise, skip this step.
2. If your hand is empty after the draw step, the players lose immediately.
3. If at least one card in your hand can be played legally, play one legal card. Otherwise, place one card face up in a shared discard pile and advance the storm marker one space.
4. Resolve the end conditions. If neither condition is met, the next player begins a turn.

A card is legal when it matches the path's color and:

- it has value 1 and the path is empty; or
- its value is higher than the top card already on that path.

Cards below a path's top card remain there but have no further effect. A completed path remains in play and cannot receive another card.

## Empty draw deck

Do not reshuffle discarded or played cards. When the draw deck is empty, players continue taking turns with the cards left in their hands. If an active player has no card after the skipped draw step, the players lose.

## End conditions

The players win immediately when value 6 is the top card on all three paths.

The players lose immediately when the storm marker reaches space 6 or an active player has no card after the draw step.

After any win or loss, stop immediately. There is no next turn or round.
